Gym plan: three short sessions instead of two long ones

Spoken, Sat 26 Sep·#fitness

Mon, Wed, Fri, 35 minutes each. Easier to keep than two long days, and it fits before work.

Plan

  • Check the gym's opening hours before 8:00
  • Pick three 35-minute routines, one per day
  • Block Mon, Wed, Fri 7:15 in the calendar
  • Review after two weeks: kept all six?
